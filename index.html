🤔 What is comfyui_ensemble_upscale?

comfyui_ensemble_upscale is a powerful image enhancement tool that works with ComfyUI (a visual AI workflow builder). This special node lets you combine up to three different ESRGAN AI models to upscale your images. Unlike traditional methods that blur or lose detail, this tool intelligently blends the strengths of multiple models to produce crisp, high-resolution images with all the original details intact.

Think of it like having three expert photo editors working together—each one brings their specialty, and the final result is better than any single one could achieve alone.

🛠️ How It Works

Using comfyui_ensemble_upscale is incredibly straightforward:

  1. Load your image into ComfyUI
  2. Add the ensemble_upscale node to your workflow
  3. Select 1-3 ESRGAN models you want to use
  4. Run the workflow and watch your image transform

The node automatically combines the outputs from all selected models using smart algorithms to keep the best parts of each result. You get higher resolution, better textures, and none of the artifacts that typically come with AI upscaling.
